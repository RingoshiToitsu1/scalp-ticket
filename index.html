Recent reads

How to read it. Every number on the ticket is recomputed here from the entry, stop and targets — the risk:reward, the stop distance, the position size. Where the model's own arithmetic disagrees with the page's, the page's figure is shown and the disagreement is flagged. A read is one model's opinion of one screenshot: it cannot see your fills, your spread, your size, or the tape between candles. It is not advice, and it is not a signal service.
